I spend a lot of time in the narrow space between Tokyo and Europe—enough, my tracker says, to have flown the circumference of Earth several times in the past few years. On certain aircraft a top-down camera points at the planet below; the live feed appears on the seat-back screen. While most passengers queue up another movie, I photograph that feed with my phone, frame after frame, 10 kilometres above the ground.

In this small book, I collected a few of my favorite photos from the chair in the sky.
