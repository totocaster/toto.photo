I made the companion zine for the [exhibition](/exhibitions/the-line-synesthesia/) with the same title. The zine is completely handmade and hand-bound. Almost every page is made from a different paper or material, including cloth, tracing, and craft paper.

The book is designed to be tactile and to spark different sensations while flipping through it for the viewer.
