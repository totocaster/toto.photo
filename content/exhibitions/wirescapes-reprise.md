A one-evening reprise of [Wirescapes](/exhibitions/wirescapes/), the 2022 duo show from UNTITLED SPACE in Tokyo, brought to Tbilisi. Urban photographs and recycled technological materials, connected again from a single power plug, this time at Mutant Radio. Saturday, 18:00–23:00.

Wirescapes is a collaboration between ByLUDO (Ludovica Cirillo) and Toto Tvalavadze.
