_Out of Memory_ is a photographic journal—a collection of snapshots, memories, textures, and moments I encounter. Every six to eight months, I print a 200-page volume. I began this series in May 2019 and intend to continue until my last day in this world. Several photographs from the series have been featured in exhibitions, including "Perspectives" (Tokyo, 2021) and "Walk With Me" (Tokyo, 2022).
